# Bot Shooter

Simple 3D first-person shooter prototype. Three.js + TypeScript + Vite. Claude is in charge of the project; the user directs it step by step.

## Commands
- `npm run dev` — dev server (http://localhost:5173). If Vite stops picking up edits (stale code in the browser) or after moving files, restart it.
- `npx tsc --noEmit` — type check

## Conventions
- Room geometry (rooms.ts): never overlap two flat faces with different colours (z-fighting flicker). Pieces must only touch (floors end exactly where the next begins, walls stop where gate pillars start). Signs are static boards (`makeSignBoard`), not Sprites (sprites swivel to face the camera).
- Weapons (pistol/rifle/shotgun) use imported .glb models (weapons/models.ts; the blocky procedural ones show until a model loads and stay as fallback; the knife is still procedural). To add or swap one: run `node scripts/optimize-models.mjs <dir>` (strips Blender camera/light/backdrop nodes, textures -> 1024 px WebP), then add a SPECS entry (rotY so the barrel points -z, length in metres, placement; `symmetrize: true` for models built on one side only, like the pistol). Characters and wolves: build them with `MeshBuilder` and merge per body part (torso/head/arms/legs = one hit-testable mesh each, ~5-8k triangles per character). Small details automatically use fewer polygons. SVG for shop pictures. Physics is hand-rolled AABB.
- To add a costume: add an entry to COSTUMES (mannequin.ts), a LOOKS entry + its torso/hat/leg details in botModel.ts.
- Weapon tuning lives only in `WEAPONS` (weapons/weapons.ts). Bot/wolf tuning at the top of their files.
- Performance rules (measured with a headless benchmark; keep them): never ray-test the terrain mesh (use `castRay` / `world.terrainRay`; the terrain is not in `world.blockers`); `world.heightAt` reads a 0.5 m height grid (bilinear), so it is cheap to call per bot per frame; per-frame collision code must use `world.boxesNear(x, z, r)` (or `nav.boxesNear`), never loop over all of `world.boxes`; rocks are InstancedMeshes (`makeRocks(...).finish()`), trees/leaves/ground cover are merged meshes; far-away bots stop casting shadows (`Mannequin.setShadows`); the HUD only writes to the DOM when a value changed (`hud.ts`); the sun shadow map is redrawn every `shadowPeriod()` frames. Draw calls ~440, triangles ~660k in the middle of the arena.
- Mutable state lives in `S` (game/state.ts): modules can't assign an imported `let`, so write `S.cash += 5`. Avoid import cycles at module top level (only call cross-module functions inside functions).
- Dev hook `window.__game` (DEV only) lets automated checks move the camera / simulate input without pointer lock.

## Game design notes
- Arena (120x120, `half = 60`, gate + entry on the south side z=+60, 24 wandering bots, up to 6 wolves): WEST = forest (about 200 trees as ONE merged mesh + one trunk mesh that blocks bullets, each trunk has a collision box; bushes, grass, flowers, stumps, logs, a giant oak, a pond with reeds, a campfire clearing, a cabin), EAST = rocky badlands (ridges, two flat-topped mesas, rock piles, boulder fields, two rock arches you can walk under, standing stones, spires, a plaster house). There are NO free-standing boxes any more except the supply-shop kiosk. Terrain: `world.heightAt(x, z)` = BUMPS + rock piles + mesas + pond basin + ridges, levelled to 0 inside `FLATS` (layout.ts). Houses (layout.ts `HOUSES`, built by `buildHouse`): 17x12 / 16x12, floor planks, walls with an open doorway (NO door: people and bots walk in and out) and windows, gable roof, chimney, a couch against the far wall, a lamp. Bots never spawn inside houses or the pond (`isFree`). Trunk / rock / wall collision = entries in `world.boxes`. Add scenery in `scenery.ts` and place it from `world.ts`; keep things out of the way of paths/houses with `keepClear`.
- Controls: WASD, Space jump (hold = higher), Ctrl run, Shift crouch (Ctrl-run then Shift = slide), right mouse = aim down sights, keys 1-4 = loadout slots (1 sidearm, 2 rifle slot, 3 heavy slot, 4 knife), Q/wheel cycle the loadout, R reload, H drink potion, E shop / terminal, Esc pause.
- Loadout (game/loadout.ts, `S.loadout`): the player carries ONE weapon per slot into the arena (+ the knife, always). Slots: sidearm = pistol or SMG, rifle = assault rifle / sniper / crossbow, heavy = shotgun or grenade launcher (empty until one is bought; the first heavy weapon bought is equipped automatically). Chosen at the ARMORY terminal in the safe room (right wall; stand near it, E, then 1 / 2 / 3 cycle the owned options of that slot; its screen shows the loadout). `switchWeapon` refuses weapons that are not equipped; the supply shop only sells ammo for equipped guns. Weapons are still bought at the weapon shop (all locked ones are listed there). Saved in the save file (v2 adds `loadout`; v1 saves are sanitized: only owned weapons in the right slots).
- New weapons (WEAPONS entries use `...BASE` defaults + optional fields: `adsSpreadMult`, `adsMoveMult`, `scope`, `pierce`, `projectile`, `explosion`, `silent`, `ammoLabel`, `shot`): SMG ($2500, auto 900 rpm, 9 dmg, weak past 14 m, hardly punished for moving); Sniper ($5500, bolt 48 rpm, 95 dmg x3 head, pierces 2 extra targets at 60% each, wild from the hip, scoped = pinpoint but sways and moving ruins it); Crossbow ($3500, silent so nobody panics, 70 dmg x3 head, 78 m/s bolts with gravity 7, 1 bolt per reload, spare "mags" = bolts at $120); Grenade Launcher ($8000, 3-chamber drum, 27 m/s bouncing grenades with 2.4 s fuse or contact, 170 dmg blast radius 6 m with falloff^1.3, knockback, hurts the player at 35% and can boost jumps). Prices are meant to sit between 1 and 3 levels of mission income (a level pays ~1100-3000 for 3-5 missions at level 1, scaling +40% per level).
- Aiming (right mouse, `S.aiming` -> `S.adsK` 0..1 over `adsTime`): FOV narrows to `adsZoom` (tan-based), spread x `adsSpreadMult` (default 0.55), walk speed x `adsMoveMult` (default 0.65), mouse sensitivity x `S.fovScale`, viewmodel moves to the centre; `scope` weapons hide the viewmodel and show the `#scope` overlay at adsK>0.9 and sway (`main.ts`). Reloading or switching cancels it.
- Physics: player (`player.ts`): step-up over ledges <= 0.6 m (camera eases via `S.camDy`), uphill slower / downhill faster, slides down slopes steeper than ~40 degrees, coyote time 0.12 s, jump buffer 0.12 s, variable jump height (release early = short hop), heavier falling (x1.25), landing dip + thud, fall damage above 17.5 m/s impact (5 per m/s, max 70), crouch-slide (0.85 s, boosted, no steering), footsteps. Bots (`Mannequin.impulse/physics`): knockback velocity `kv` + `air` height, stun suspends the AI, dead bodies keep flying; hits push bots by `impactImpulse * 0.06` m/s (shotgun blasts throw people back), explosions much more. `projectiles.ts` bodies collide with the height field and `world.boxes` (restitution, friction). Characters are solid for the player (`physics.ts`).
- UI (main.css): dark glass panels (`.panel`), green accent, gold for money/keys, red for danger; CSS variables at the top. HUD: score card (top left), missions with progress bars (top right), health + potions (bottom left), ammo with reload bar (bottom right), loadout slots (bottom centre), crosshair ticks that open with the weapon's spread (`setCrosshair`). Pause menu: Play/Resume, Reload map, Settings, Rules, New game, mute; dying turns it red ("You died" + stat chips). Settings (sensitivity, volume, graphics High/Medium/Low) are saved in `botshooter.settings.v1`.
- Water: the pond (`POND` in layout.ts) slows the player (up to 40%), splashes, lower jumps, and a landing in it does no fall damage; bots do not spawn in it. Wolves and bots share the same knockback physics (`impulse`, stun).
- Knife (`melee: true` in WEAPONS, always owned): no ammo/reload/shop entry (the shop lists only `shopWeapons` = non-melee), 2.4 m reach, 3 rays over a +-11 degree arc, 40 dmg (x2 head), swish sound, slash animation (`swingT`).
- Bot combat (costume fields `combat`, `zone`, `speedMul` in COSTUMES): Cowboy (`ranged`) is harmless until shot (nearby cowboys join in via `alertPack`), then keeps ~13 m away, circles, needs line of sight (`hooks.lineOfSight` raycasts world.blockers) and shoots (9 dmg, accuracy drops with distance). Criminal (`melee`, `zone: 'edge'`) spawns/patrols in the band 5-14 m from the walls (`world.randomEdgePoint`), attacks automatically within 36 m, stabs for 10 dmg, gives up after 3 s beyond 52 m, then ignores the player for 8 s. Sporty x1.7 and Superman x1.9 walking speed. `BotHooks` (mannequin.ts) is how bots reach the player (damage, tracers, sounds).
- Fearless costumes (`fearless: true`: cowboy, soldier, ninja, superman) are never scared: no flee, no retreat, immune to panic. A shot soldier (harmless) turns and stares at the player for 6 s (`alertT`). Criminals walk at 4.2 m/s when chasing. Superman (`combat: 'melee'`, `SUPERMAN_PUNCH`, `provoked: true`): calm until shot, then walks up (6.4 m/s), winds up 0.6 s (arm pulled back; the blow only lands if the player is still within ~2.8 m), hits for 45, then rests `interval` = 4.2 s (bent over, heaving) before the next punch: the player's window to shoot back. Only criminals (not fearless) retreat when badly hurt.
- Civilian AI (harmless costumes, `Mannequin.civilian`, modes calm/startle/flee/cower): shot => `panic(true)`: freeze 0.1-0.3 s with hands up (+ scream, `hooks.scream`), then run away at ~5.8 m/s (never faster than 1.3x; the player sprints at 9) steering around crates/walls (`pickRunHeading` probes 8 directions), 14-22 s of fear; cornered => cower. Gunshots (`Mannequin.scareNear` from `fire()`, 16 m, shotgun 24 m), deaths (26 m) and running neighbours (contagion, 9 m) panic others for 7-12 s. After calming down a bot stays `wary` 10-16 s (runs again if the player comes within 14 m). Bravery (random + soldier/builder/superman bonus) lengthens the freeze and shortens fear. Wounded (<45% HP) bots limp and hunch. Calm bots: follow the player with their head within 11 m, idle gestures (stretch, watch, hips, wave, scratch), step away from a player closer than 2 m, keep apart from each other (`separate`). Armed bots: cowboys wait ~0.5-0.9 s before the first shot, sometimes fire a quick double shot, strafe at varying speeds and sidestep when hit; criminals step back after a stab (hit and run); ninjas zig-zag; criminals (the only armed bots that are not fearless) `retreat` (run for cover 3-5 s, once per life) when below 20% HP. Dying: falls backwards with arms flung (group Euler order is YXZ), lies ~2.5 s, shrinks.
- Scoring: 10 per landed shot (once per shot, not per pellet), kill = costume points (+50 headshot kill). Cash = same amounts, spent at the shop. The pause-menu DEV cheat buttons (free cash, skip level, jump to boss / horde) were removed; use `window.__game` for testing instead.
- Ammo is magazine-based: `Weapon.ammo` + `Weapon.spare[]` (max 6 spare; reload takes the fullest spare and DISCARDS the loaded magazine, whatever is left in it). Shotgun is locked at the start (`unlockPrice: 3000`, comes loaded + 1 spare); once owned its shop entry sells shells.
- Player: 100 HP, NO natural regeneration. Health potions ($250, heal 50, carry 3) bought at the shop, drunk with H. Death screen with "Play again".
- Shops (game/shops.ts): WEAPON shop in the safe room (sells every locked gun) and SUPPLY shop beside the arena gate (magazines for owned guns + potion). Stand in the green circle -> "Press E" -> E opens the window -> press the item's number.
- Level flow (phase in game/flow.ts, updateFlow): safe room (z 80-107) --gate1 (z=80)--> airlock (z 60-80) --gate2 (z=60)--> arena. Walk 4 m into the airlock: gate1 seals -> 'loading' (startLevel() builds the level behind closed doors, 2.6 s) -> gate2 opens -> entering the arena closes gate2 behind you. 3 finished missions (MISSIONS_TO_FINISH) -> levelComplete(): gate2 opens + bright exit light; entering the airlock seals it, level++, gate1 opens (0.6 s beat, no loading screen). Bots/wolves are only simulated while renaActive(). Characters have a minLevel in COSTUMES (criminal 4, cowboy 5, soldier 7, superman 10, ninja 15; used by levelConfig.allowed); wolves from level 3 (2 of them, +1 every 3 levels, max 6); levels 1-2 are civilians only; the level that introduces a character guarantees at least 2 of it. Level building is queued (levelWork, 2 items per frame while the doors are shut): no freeze, no loading screen. Missions: 5 per level, first is always a score target, harder and better paid each; rewards scale with level; startLevel forces the characters the missions need to exist (orceCostume).
- Name tag (game/nametag.ts): 10 Hz raycast from the crosshair, shows name + HP, red for hostile.
- Wolves: calm wanderers until shot, then chase at 7.5 m/s and bite (12 dmg/s); packmates within 12m join in; give up if the player stays >45m away for 6s. 30s respawn far from the player. Bots and wolves share the Target interface (`label`, `points`, `damage`, `hitMeshes`).
- Esc: first Play goes fullscreen + `navigator.keyboard.lock(['Escape'])` (Chromium) so the page receives Esc itself: Esc closes the shop, else pauses via `document.exitPointerLock()` (script exit => Esc can re-capture the mouse without a click). Fallback (no keyboard lock): the browser releases the mouse on Esc and doesn't deliver that keydown while locked. Esc in-game -> pointerlockchange: shop open => close it ("soft pause", any key/click continues), else pause menu; Esc in the menu calls startPlaying() (may be refused right after an Esc; Resume always works).
- Sounds: pistol one-shot, rifle looped burst, shotgun blast + pump 0.4s later, reload sounds for pistol/rifle (reloadTime matches: 1.15s / 2.4s). Wolf growl, hurt and potion sounds are synthesized. Mute button in the pause menu (remembered in localStorage).

- Saved progress (game/save.ts, localStorage key `botshooter.save.v1`, data version `v: 2`): ONLY level, cash, the loadout, and each weapon's owned/ammo/spare magazines. Written once a second when something changed + on pagehide/hidden; read once at start (`loadProgress`: clamps bad values, ignores unknown weapons and other versions). Health, potions, score, arena and missions are NOT saved. `resetGame()` keeps the save (Reload map / Play again); `resetGame(true)` + `clearSave()` is the "New game" button. If you change the save shape, bump `v` and handle the old version in `loadProgress`.

## Status
Playable prototype with a level system: safe room (weapon shop + armory terminal), airlock, a forest/badlands arena with two houses, 5 missions per level, shops, health + potions, bots, wolves, rules window.

- Armed characters are limited: `COSTUME_CAP` (mannequin.ts) allows at most 2 criminals and 2 cowboys alive at once; after one is killed that costume cannot spawn for 25 s (`cooldownUntil`, cleared at level start).

- Performance pass 2 (measured: triangles per frame without bots 521k -> 231k): scenery meshes are split into cells (`ChunkedBuilder`, terrain in 4x4 chunks) so view culling works; grass/bush/flower cells are only visible within ~62 m of the player (`world.update`); leaf/bush/flower balls use low `ball(..., 'mid'|'low')` detail; the sun shadow box follows the player (`world.followSun`, 84 m, snapped to texels; 2048 map on High); only the point lights of the zone the player is in are on (`updateZoneLights` in main.ts; shader variants pre-compiled at startup; the explosion light exists from the start so no recompile happens mid-game); dynamic resolution (`tuneResolution` in settings.ts) lowers the render scale when frames take > 19 ms and raises it again when there is spare time (disabled in automated browsers). Never add or remove a light at runtime (shader recompile hitch): toggle `visible` among pre-compiled sets instead.

- Campfire (`campfireBurn` in player.ts): standing within 0.95 m of the fire (below 1.3 m above it) hurts 5 HP/s. Sniper audio: `sniper-shot.mp3` (the user's recording, echo tail cut at 1.3 s) `sniper-bolt.mp3` (the bolt being worked, played 0.45 s after EVERY shot like the shotgun pump) and `sniper-reload.mp3` (the user's uzi-reload recording, 3.1 s, played when a reload starts).

- Arena edge: walls are 10 m high (the player can't jump them), the ground is flat in a belt ~3-14 m wide along the walls, hills/ridges/mesas are scaled by `HILL` = 0.7 in world.ts (highest point ~3.9 m), and `keepInArena()` in main.ts clamps the player inside as a safety net.

- Difficulty curve (missions.ts, levels 1-25; difficulty is capped at 25, later levels repeat it): intros 3 wolves, 4 criminals, 5 cowboys, 7 soldiers, 10 Superman, 15 ninjas (`INTRO_NOTES` are the level banners). Mission targets scale by `1 + 0.1(L-1)` (level 1: 300 points, 4 kills, 2 kills of one type), rewards by `1 + 0.18(L-1)`; kill missions only name characters that exist on that level and armed-character kill targets are capped at 4.

- GROWING ARENA (layout.ts `STAGES`, `stageForLevel`, `stageOf`; world.ts `setStage`, `world.region`): the map is built once at full size (120x120) but only part of it is in play. 7 stages, from level 1 / 3 / 6 / 10 / 15 / 20 / 25: level 1 is 40x36 m next to the gate (10 bots), level 25 the whole map (24 bots). Stages are nested rectangles (the south edge z = 60 with the gate never moves). `startLevel` (flow.ts) calls `world.setStage` behind the closed doors: the walls are rebuilt (`buildWalls`, boxes + BoxGrid refreshed), scenery that lies outside is hidden (each scenery piece belongs to the first stage containing it: per-stage `stageGroups`, trees/cover chunked per stage, instanced rocks sorted by stage and drawn as a prefix via `mesh.count`), unused bots are `disable()`d, the banner says "The arena has grown!". Bots, wolves, spawns and the player are clamped to `world.region` (never use `world.half` for bounds; it is the size of the whole map). Houses/pond/camp are placed with `ctxAt(x, z)` so they appear with their stage. When you add a landmark, keep stage edges from cutting through it (check STAGES) and build it with `ctxAt`. Houses: (-38,-8) and (32,22).

- LEVELS 1-20 + BOSS (changed from 25): `MAX_LEVEL = 20` (missions.ts); stages start at levels 1/3/6/9/13/17/20 (level 20 = full map); wolves 3, criminals 4, cowboys 5, soldiers 7, Superman 10, ninjas 14. Level 21 (and every level after it: rematches, boss health +25% per rematch) is the BOSS level (`levelConfig(level).boss`): one mission "Defeat the Colossus" ($15000), no regular bots/wolves, full map, victory banner (`levelComplete` in flow.ts). The boss is a Mannequin with costume `boss` (mannequin.ts: scale 2.1, 2400 HP, violet skin + horns in botModel.ts) driven by `bossBrain`: keeps 17-32 m away (flees at 4.4 m/s when you come closer), every 6-8.5 s (3.5-5 s below half health) throws its arms back for 1 s and calls 2 (3) zombies via `hooks.summon` (max 6 alive, 10 in rage), when you get within 6.5 m it pants for 3.2 s (bent over, only swats for 7 dmg, 9 s cooldown) = your window. Zombies = pooled Mannequins (`zombies` in actors.ts, 12, `oneLife`, costume `zombie`: 70 HP, 7 dmg claw, 3.3 m/s, always hunting). `actors.ts`: `regulars` (24 ordinary bots, what flow.ts scales per stage), `boss`, `zombies`, `mannequins` = all of them (use this for hit tests). Boss HP bar = `#bossBar` (index.html/main.css, `updateBossBar` in main.ts). Boss/zombies are switched off (`disable()`) on every other level; costumes with `weight 0` / `minLevel 999` never spawn normally.

- BOSS UPDATE: zombies are a LIMITED pool (8, `zombies` in actors.ts). A cast puts 2 (3 in rage) zombies 6-9 m from the PLAYER (not the boss); when all 8 are out, `hooks.summon` makes the zombie farthest from the player vanish and reuses it. Within 10 m of the boss its summon timer drops to 0.8 s (it reacts to being crowded). Within 5 m it punches (15 dmg, every 1.3 s, range 4.6 m); the 3.2 s panting still happens when you run up to it (cooldown 9 s). 24-85 m away it throws boulders every 4.5-6.5 s (1.2 s wind-up with the arm thrown back; `spawnBossRock` in projectiles.ts via `botHooks.throwRock` set in main.ts): lobbed arc, flight 1.5-3.4 s with a small lead on a moving player, a direct hit does 60 damage + shake, a miss throws up debris. Tunables: `BOSS` / `BOSS_SWAT` in mannequin.ts, `ROCK_*` in projectiles.ts.

- Fleeing (`pickRunHeading` / `runFrom` in mannequin.ts): runners score 10 headings by distance from the threat, `clearance` (probes every 0.6 m, so thin house walls are seen), a `wallPenalty` (anything within 7 m of the arena walls, corners count double: they run ALONG walls instead of into corners), momentum and distance gained. Pressed against something for 0.2 s => escape mode (1.3 s): heads toward open ground, ignoring momentum. Civilians only cower when truly pinned (<4 m from the player). Measured in a headless test: 0-3% of fleeing bots stuck, none in corners (before: up to 83% in the small arena).

- VOICES: recordings live in `public/audio/voices/<costume id>-<hit|death|casual|summon|throw|pant>-<n>.mp3` (mono, trimmed, normalised to about -18 LUFS with ffmpeg: `highpass 70 Hz, silenceremove both ends, loudnorm`) and are listed in `public/audio/voices/manifest.json`, generated by `npm run voices` (scripts/voices-manifest.mjs) after adding files. `voiceLine(id, kind, distance)` in audio.ts picks a random line (slight pitch variation, quieter with distance, silent beyond 40 m); casual chatter never overlaps another voice, 'hit' lines are limited (one per 0.7 s overall, max 2 at once) and drama (hit/death) cuts small talk off. Triggers (mannequin.ts, via `hooks.voice`): a survived hit (per bot at most every 1.6 s), death, casual chatter every 14-30 s while calm and the player is within 16 m (`chatter`), and for the boss: summon / throw / pant. Characters without recordings are silent. Regular: hit = "Ow", "Dentist appointment"; casual = "Hearing a gun", "Forgot why I walked", "Nice weather". Regular also has casual 'Please behave yourself' and death 'Should have stayed home today'. Winter: casual 'just my personality', 'wind whispering', 'winter is coming'. Winter also has hit ('my only jacket', 'snowball is kinder') and death ('long night arrived', 'tell the snow'); Builder casual 'safety inspection', 'on my brake', hit 'coming out of deposit', 'hard hat / armoured hat', 'watch the vest', death 'finish the roof'. Sporty: casual 'protein shake', 'push-ups before breakfast', 'run away from problems', hit 'I run marathons', 'my knee', death 'have not stretched'. - DOWNLOADED CHARACTERS (entities/glbRig.ts): the boss is a low-poly armoured knight (9000 triangles, Sketchfab model simplified with meshoptimizer) and each summoned zombie is a random one of the 10 PolyArt zombies (about 2000 triangles, one 256 px palette).
